# Image credit records

Each item in `public/images/credits.json` has `file`, `caseId`, `sourceSite`, `directPageUrl`, `title`, `authorOrCredit`, `licenseName`, `licenseUrl`, `dateAccessed`, `diagnosisAsStated`, `fitzpatrickType` (nullable), and `modificationsMade`. Source URLs and license URLs may be `null` when they were not supplied or verified. Do not invent an author, source, or open-license claim.
